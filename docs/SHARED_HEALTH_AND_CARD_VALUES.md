# Shared health, card values and free wands (2026-10-30)

Fortitude now increases permanent player HP as well as structure HP by 15%
per common-card power. The player multiplier derives from persisted card
power, instead of incrementing an additional saved flat-health field. Acquiring
the card heals only the newly gained capacity; temporary HP remains outside
the multiplier. Class and flat HP bonuses remain part of permanent capacity.

Both player and building shrines can offer Fortitude. Their shared HP powers
add before applying the shrine multiplier, independently of tome power. The
cards show both Max HP and Building HP. Legacy Vitality shrine state remains
readable, retaining its flat player HP and receiving the paired structure
bonus; that retired card is not reintroduced into the offer pool.

Tesla Attack count compares resolved combat counts, including its native
attack and existing global/local modifiers. A first count card shows 1 -> 2.
Cards labelled Extra projectiles continue to show the additional count.
Axe/pickaxe cards show Power percentages instead of a weapon-level stat line,
using the same +35 percentage-point level progression as harvesting.

Both wands are starter content for new and existing profiles. Their old
quests retain stable IDs and silver rewards but no longer sell an unlock.
Ten paid exotic discoveries remain; the highest purchase milestone therefore
requires ten purchases. Toggler's eight-purchase gate remains reachable.
Existing paid history, toggle settings and progression are preserved.
